Architecture

Why this needs AI, and where it stops

Two separate layers, on purpose — one mechanical, one linguistic. Neither pretends to be the other.
Layer 1 — Rules

Tradeoff scoring

Deterministic and auditable. Same inputs always produce the same scores. This is where the actual "reasoning structure" lives — four lenses × three time horizons, with confidence bands instead of fake precision. No hallucination risk because nothing here is generated.

Layer 2 — Optional LLM

Plain-language explanation

The rules engine can't read "my parents really want me to play it safe" in your free-text box and adjust its tone. An LLM can. It only ever explains the numbers already computed — it's instructed never to override them, and the app works fully without it.

Human-in-the-loop

The decision itself

Pathwise will never output a field called "recommendation." It computes a weighted lean, always shows the runner-up's strongest dimension, and the actual choice — the one with real consequences — stays entirely with you.
